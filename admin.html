A complete guide to the admin panel

                    

                        This panel is how you turn a recorded lesson video into the interactive lesson students actually use. Students never see this screen. Everything you save here is live for them immediately. You do not edit code. You pick a lesson, attach a video, let the panel find the pauses and chapters, check the chapter matches, and save.
                    


                    
What a finished lesson is

                    

                        A lesson on the student menu is one video plus a clickable chapter list. The student can watch straight through, or click a chapter to jump to that part. At certain moments the video pauses and waits for a click before continuing. Some sections replay until the student clicks. Those behaviors are not guessed at playback time. They come from this panel, from the list you save for that lesson.
                    

                    

                        Each lesson exists in two independent versions: With Text (words appear on screen) and Without Text (no on-screen words). They are separate videos, separate scans, and separate saves. Finishing one does not change the other. The left sidebar switch decides which version you are editing. Set it before you upload, attach, scan, or save.
                    


                    
How the colored screens in the video work

                    

                        The lesson videos already contain full-screen colored frames. Those frames are instructions to the player. This panel finds them when you click Scan Video. You do not place them here by hand.
                    

                    
    
                        
  • Yellow means pause. Playback stops and waits until the student clicks, then continues.
    • 
                        
  • Green means a chapter starts. It also pauses. This is the spot a menu chapter can jump to.
    • 
                        
  • Red means replay. Playback goes back to the previous pause and loops that stretch until the student clicks.
    • 
                    

                    

                        Scan finds where those things happen. It does not decide which menu chapter each green screen belongs to. That is what Auto-Match Chapters is for. Both pieces have to be saved or students will not get the full interactive lesson.
                    


                    
Logging in

                    

                        The login screen is only for people who set up lessons. Students never use it. Sign in with the email and password for this project.
                    

                    
    
                        
  • Remember Me keeps the email and password filled on this browser so you are not typing them every time. Only use it on a computer you trust.
    • 
                        
  • Forgot password? sends a reset email to the address in the email field.
    • 
                        
  • Back to Main Menu leaves admin and opens the student menu without logging in.
    • 
                    

                    

                        After a successful login, the dashboard opens and the lesson list starts loading on its own. You should not need to click anything before you see lessons on the left. Stay logged in while you work. If you are signed out, saves will fail.
                    


                    
The top bar

                    

                        The dark bar across the top is navigation out of the working area. None of these buttons attach a video or scan one. They open help, history, the menu editor, the live student menu, or your account.
                    

                    
    
                        
  • How To Use This opens this guide full-screen. Minimize keeps it in a side tray while you work. Expand brings it back to full-screen. × closes it.
    • 
                        
  • What's New is a running list of what changed in the panel. It is history, not instructions.
    • 
                        
  • Roadmap is what is planned next, plus contact details if something is broken.
    • 
                        
  • Edit Menu changes the student menu itself: order, names, which lessons show, which section they sit in. That is separate from attaching videos. See the Edit Menu section below.
    • 
                        
  • View Student Menu opens the live menu students use, in a new tab. Use it after you save, to click a couple of chapters and confirm they jump to the right spots and the pauses feel right.
    • 
                        
  • My Account shows the email you are signed in as, and is where you log out.
    • 
                    


                    
Load Lessons & Videos, and Upload Video

                    

                        The compact bar under the header is for bringing files and the lesson list into the panel.
                    

                    

                        Load Lessons & Videos rebuilds the left-hand list from the current menu and your uploaded videos. It also refreshes the Available Videos section. This now runs automatically when you enter the dashboard. Click it again when you have uploaded new files, changed the menu, or the list looks stale. Lessons that still need a video are easy to spot in the tree (and with the Needs Video filter).
                    

                    

                        Upload Video opens a file picker for one or more MP4 files from your computer. A batch of files is queued: a few upload at a time (not all at once), and each finished upload starts its own scan automatically. A corner panel shows per-file status and a live time estimate for the remaining uploads and scans. You do not need to click Scan Video for files in that batch.
                    

                    

                        Uploading does not attach the file to a lesson by itself, except when the file name matches a lesson (or you upload a single file with a lesson already selected). Otherwise you still pick the lesson and use Assign. Name a Without Text file so it ends in _x (for example cleavage_stage_x.mp4). That puts it in the Without Text group. Files that do not end in _x go with the With Text videos.
                    

                    

                        Progress and results still appear as a small message in the bottom-right corner. For a multi-file upload, watch the Video ingest panel instead of clicking Upload Video again. Scanning a long video can take several minutes.
                    


                    
The left sidebar: finding and choosing a lesson

                    

                        The sidebar is the table of contents for this panel. You always work on one lesson at a time, for one version at a time. Click a lesson name to select it. The gray highlight is the selected lesson. Everything on the right then belongs to that lesson and that version.
                    

                    
Which version?

                    

                        With Text and Without Text switch which copy of every lesson you are editing. The tree, the attached video, the pause list, and the chapter links are all per version. If you attach a With Text video while Without Text is selected, you have attached it to the wrong copy. Set this first, every time you sit down.
                    

                    
Find a lesson

                    

                        Type part of a name to hide lessons that do not match. This only filters the list. It does not rename anything.
                    

                    
Show

                    

                        All shows every lesson. Needs Video shows only lessons that still have no video attached for the current version. Has Video shows only lessons that already have one. Use Needs Video when you are working through a backlog of uploads.
                    

                    
The lesson list

                    

                        Lessons are grouped by section, the same sections students see on the menu. Click a section title to collapse or expand it. A colored dot next to a lesson name is status: one color means a video is attached, the other means it still needs one. If a lesson or section is hidden from students via Edit Menu, it still appears here so you can work on it, usually with a Hidden mark.
                    

                    

                        Some sections have a name field. Change it, then save from the box that appears in the bottom-right corner. That renames the section as it appears in the menu. It does not delete the section or move lessons.
                    

                    

                        The small arrow at the top of the sidebar collapses the whole list so the working area on the right is wider.
                    


                    
Lesson & Video

                    

                        This card is the identity of the selected lesson: what it is called, whether it has a video, and which file is attached. Collapse the header if you want more room for the pause list below.
                    

                    

                        Until you click a lesson, this card is empty. After you click one you will see the display name, a Has Video or Needs Video label, the section it belongs to, the original name if you renamed it, and the current video file if one is attached.
                    

                    
Lesson Name

                    

                        This is the name students see in the menu. Change the text, then save from the box in the bottom-right corner. Renaming is safe. It does not change the video, the pauses, or the chapter links. It also does not move the lesson to a different section. Use Edit Menu if you need to move or hide it.
                    

                    
Attach a video

                    

                        A lesson plays whichever file you assign here. Use the dropdown to pick from videos already uploaded, then click Assign (or Update if one is already attached). Assigning a different file does not by itself rebuild pauses and chapters. After you attach or change a video, scan it.
                    

                    

                        If the dropdown is empty, upload first, then click Load Lessons & Videos if the new file does not appear.
                    


                    
Video Pauses & Chapters

                    

                        This is the heart of the panel. The attached video is just footage until this list exists. The list is every pause, chapter start, and replay, in time order. The student player follows this list. If it is empty, the lesson is a plain video with no interactive pauses and no working chapter jumps.
                    

                    
Scan Video

                    

                        Scan reads the attached file, finds the yellow, green, and red screens, and builds the list for you. You should not be typing timestamps by hand for a normal lesson. Scanning again rebuilds the list from scratch from the current video. That can overwrite a list you already saved for this version, so do not scan again unless you mean to replace it.
                    

                    

                        Scanning takes time. The bottom-right message tells you it is working. Stay on the page until it finishes.
                    

                    
The list itself

                    

                        Each row is one event. You will see what it does (pause, chapter, replay), when it starts, and when it ends, in seconds. After a scan you can look down the list and see whether the shape of the lesson looks right: chapters where you expect them, pauses between them, replays where a segment should loop.
                    

                    
Auto-Match Chapters

                    

                        Scan knows a green screen is “a chapter,” but not that it is “Cleavage” or “Gastrulation.” Auto-Match Chapters has the system read the title on each chapter screen in the video and match it to a chapter in the student menu. It fills in the Chapter Matches list. It can be wrong. You are expected to check it.
                    

                    

                        The status line under the buttons tells you whether matching has been run, is running, succeeded, or failed.
                    

                    
Chapter Matches

                    

                        Each row is one title screen the system found. You will see when it appears, the title it read, the menu chapter it guessed, and how sure it was. The dropdown is the important control. Pick the correct menu chapter, or set the row to none if that screen should not link anywhere.
                    

                    

                        The reviewed checkbox is only a note to yourself. It does not change student playback. Saving is what matters.
                    

                    
Save (bottom-right)

                    

                        There is one Save for the whole panel. When you change a lesson name, a section name, the pause list, or chapter matches, a box appears in the bottom-right corner — the same place as the loading messages — and asks you to save. Click that Save. Until you do, students still have the previous version.
                    

                    

                        That single save writes everything that is pending: chapter links (so a student clicking a chapter jumps to the right time) and the pause / replay list (so the video actually pauses and loops). You do not hunt for separate Save buttons.
                    

                    
Clear & Start Over

                    

                        This erases the pause and chapter list for this lesson version so you can scan again from a clean slate. The attached video stays. Use it when the list is empty, garbled, leftover from a different file, or you attached the right video after a bad scan. Then scan again, match chapters again, and save from the bottom-right box.
                    


                    
Available Videos

                    

                        This is a library of files you have uploaded, not the lesson list. Open it to see With Text and Without Text groups, with counts. Preview plays a file in a pop-up without attaching it to a lesson. Use preview to confirm you have the right file before you assign it.
                    


                    
Edit Menu

                    

                        Edit Menu changes the student menu structure. It does not attach videos or scan them. Changes apply to both the With Text and Without Text menus. Nothing is permanently deleted: hidden items can be shown again, and Reset to default restores the original order from the built-in menu.
                    

                    
    
                        
  • Reorder with the up and down controls on a section or lesson.
    • 
                        
  • Rename by editing the name field.
    • 
                        
  • Hide / show so a lesson or section stays in the system but does not appear for students. You can still select hidden lessons in the admin sidebar.
    • 
                        
  • Move a lesson to another section with the dropdown on that row.
    • 
                        
  • + Add section creates an empty section you can rename and move lessons into.
    • 
                        
  • Reset to default throws away the overlay and restores the original menu. This is not a small undo. Do not use it unless you mean it.
    • 
                        
  • Save in the Edit Menu footer is what actually publishes the structure. If you close the window without saving, those edits are not live.
    • 
                    


                    
What students see after you save

                    

                        Saves are live. There is no separate publish step. When a student opens that lesson version they get:
                    

                    
    
                        
  • The video you attached.
    • 
                        
  • Pauses at Yellow and Green screens, continuing on click.
    • 
                        
  • Replays at Red screens, until they click.
    • 
                        
  • Chapter menu items that jump to the times you saved in Chapter Matches, for every row that is not set to none.
    • 
                    

                    

                        Always spot-check with View Student Menu after the first save on a lesson. Click two or three chapters, let a pause happen, and confirm a replay if the lesson has one.
                    


                    
The messages in the corner

                    

                        The bottom-right pop-up is the status of the current task: loading the list, uploading, scanning, matching, saving, or an error. It stays visible while you scroll. If something fails, read that message before clicking again. Long scans are normal. Repeated clicks can stack work and make a mess.
                    

                    

                        When you have unsaved edits, a Save your changes? box appears in that same corner. That is the only save you need for the dashboard. (Edit Menu still has its own Save inside that window.)
                    


                    
Developer Tools

                    

                        The button at the very bottom of the page turns on extra technical controls for troubleshooting. Leave it off for normal work. You do not need it to attach videos, scan, match chapters, edit the menu, or save.
                    

                    

                        On a lesson video itself (not this admin page), Shift+D turns the marker detector overlay on or off. It is off until you press that shortcut. The same note is at the bottom of this panel so you can find it later.
                    


                    
If something looks wrong

                    
    
                        
  • A chapter jumps to the wrong place. Select the lesson, open Chapter Matches, fix that row’s dropdown, then save from the bottom-right box. Spot-check the student menu.
    • 
                        
  • The pause list is empty or obviously wrong. Confirm the right video is attached and the right version is selected. Click Clear & Start Over, then Scan Video, then Auto-Match Chapters, then save from the bottom-right box.
    • 
                        
  • You attached the wrong video. Assign the correct one, then scan and match again. The old list belongs to the old file.
    • 
                        
  • You edited the Without Text copy by accident. Switch Which version?, then do the work again on the correct copy. The two versions do not copy from each other.
    • 
                        
  • Pauses work but chapter clicks do not, or the reverse. You likely still have unsaved edits. Use the Save box in the bottom-right corner.
    • 
                        
  • The left list is empty after login. Wait for the corner message to finish. If it errors, click Load Lessons & Videos. Stay logged in.
    • 
                        
  • A new upload does not appear in the dropdown. Click Load Lessons & Videos. Confirm the file was an MP4 and, for Without Text, that the name ends in _x.
    • 
                        
  • A save failed. You were probably signed out. Log in and save again. The student lesson does not change until a save succeeds.
    •

The sidebar tree mirrors the live order and flags hidden items.
